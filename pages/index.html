Why SRVC Hockey Camp

Leadership-Driven Coaching

Our staff includes former military leaders and experienced coaches who teach discipline, accountability, and resilience alongside hockey skills.

Serious Skill Development

Players sharpen skating, shooting, puck control, and game IQ through structured drills designed to translate directly into game performance.

Character First, Hockey Second

We focus on building athletes who lead in the locker room, classroom, and community. Respect, integrity, and teamwork are non‑negotiable.

Program Highlights

  • Small‑group instruction with focused feedback
  • On‑ice and off‑ice training sessions
  • Strength, conditioning, and agility work
  • Leadership and mindset development sessions
  • Daily emphasis on discipline, effort, and attitude
  • Safe, structured, and positive environment

Our Mission

SRVC Hockey exists to develop disciplined, confident, and resilient young athletes through elite hockey instruction and character‑driven leadership training.
