Why SRVC Hockey Camp

Know the Standard

Success starts with a blueprint. Our camp begins by defining the technical and tactical standards used by top-tier programs like USA Hockey and Hockey Canada. We don't just 'play hockey'; we teach the specific habits that build the foundation for long-term development.

Meet the Standard

It’s one thing to do a drill; it’s another to execute it under pressure. Our curriculum focuses on high-repetition, high-intensity training designed to turn new skills into second nature. We bridge the gap between 'knowing' and 'doing' through data-driven feedback and accountability.

Exceed the Standard

The best players don’t just meet expectations; they shatter them. Our camp environment is designed to foster the 'plus-one' mentality—the extra effort, the creative playmaking, and the leadership that changes the outcome of a game. We give you the tools to not only compete but to dominate.

Program Highlights

  • Small‑group instruction with focused feedback
  • On‑ice and off‑ice training sessions
  • Strength, conditioning, and agility work
  • Leadership and mindset development sessions
  • Daily emphasis on discipline, effort, and attitude
  • Safe, structured, and positive environment

Our Mission

SRVC Hockey exists to develop disciplined, confident, and resilient young athletes through elite hockey instruction and character‑driven leadership training.
