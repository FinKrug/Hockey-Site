Our Leadership Team

SRVC Hockey was founded by three teammates and West Point graduates who served together as elite military leaders and shared a lifelong passion for the game. Through their diverse careers in special operations, professional hockey front offices, and global corporate leadership, they identified a critical gap in youth sports: the space between "wanting to win" and "preparing to win". Realizing that each brought a unique set of skills to the table—from elite physiological conditioning to pro-level scouting strategy—they united to provide athletes with a tactical roadmap for excellence that translates potential into predictable, high-level results.

KC Finnegan

KC Finnegan is a 2001 West Point graduate and former NCAA Division I forward whose career is defined by elite leadership and a mastery of human performance. After serving over nine years as a Special Forces Commander and earning high-level decorations for leadership and valor, he spent a decade in the Middle East as a Senior Military Advisor. As an entrepreneur who has founded and sold multiple fitness studios globally, KC combines disciplined organizational leadership with high-level strength and conditioning expertise. Currently raising three elite athletes competing at the NCAA and Major Junior levels, he offers SRVC players a unique roadmap for navigating the highest tiers of the sport.

At SRVC, KC applies a tactical, performance-driven framework to bridge the gap between "wanting to win" and "preparing to win" by treating the habit-stacking of Attitude, Preparation, and Effort as the mission-essential foundation of an athlete’s development. Leveraging his expertise in Special Forces leadership and elite human performance, he mentors players to master each pillar through the lens of disciplined routines and the high-stakes accountability of a Special Operations Commander. This methodology cultivates a relentless work ethic and a challenge-oriented perspective, ensuring that an athlete’s physical talent is reinforced by a durable mental toughness and a professional approach to the game.

Eric Joyce

Eric Joyce is a 2002 West Point graduate and former NCAA Division I defenseman for the Black Knights who transitioned from a rugged on-ice career to elite organizational leadership. A highly decorated Army Captain, he served in key international leadership roles before earning a Master’s degree from Harvard’s Kennedy School and working in strategic advisory positions for both the public and private sectors. Eric has since built an extensive NHL front-office career, including seven years as Assistant General Manager for the Florida Panthers, a tenure as Director of Hockey Strategy for the Toronto Maple Leafs, and his current role as a Pro Scout for the Carolina Hurricanes.

At SRVC, Eric applies a standards-informed, evidence-based framework to transform physical skill into elite performance by treating Attitude, Preparation, and Effort as the critical architecture of an athlete’s success. Leveraging his background in counter-terrorism and professional hockey operations, he mentors players to master each pillar by approaching every repetition prepared, with precision, and with the urgency of a championship moment. This methodology builds a specialized mental toughness and strategic composure, ensuring that an athlete’s technical ability is always backed by a mental/emotional competitive edge.

Kevin Emore

Kevin Emore is a 2003 West Point graduate and DI Defenseman who served as a highly decorated Field Artillery Officer across several global theaters. His distinguished civilian career has spanned the globe, providing strategic oversight on complex global issues in both the public and private sectors. Following a tenure in government advisory roles, he transitioned to Nike, where he led high-performance wellness and behavioral change programming for one of the world's most iconic athletic brands. Kevin brings this expertise in large-scale habit formation and elite preparation to SRVC, helping athletes build the disciplined routines necessary to sustain high-level performance.

At SRVC, Kevin applies a strategic, behavioral-science framework to the development of elite habits, treating the optimization of Nutrition, Recovery, and Mental Conditioning as the essential engine of an athlete's performance. Leveraging his background in global operations and his experience leading large-scale fitness programming at Nike, he mentors players to master the "Preparation" pillar through the lens of consistent, small habits that lead to major readiness. This methodology drives meaningful, long-term behavior change and a growth mindset, ensuring that an athlete’s physical output is sustained by a professional-grade approach to wellness and recovery.
