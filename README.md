# SRVC Hockey Registration Website

I was contracted by SRVC Hockey to design and develop a registration website for their hockey camp over a two-month period. The primary objective was to streamline the registration process for both parents and organizers while providing a reliable way to manage participant information.

The website collected registration details from players and their parents through a user-friendly online form. Once submitted, the information was automatically synced to Google Sheets, allowing organizers to efficiently review, manage, and organize registrations without manual data entry.

In addition to registration management, I integrated Square's payment processing system so families could securely pay camp fees directly through the website. This created a seamless registration experience while simplifying payment tracking for the organization.

This project gave me valuable experience working with real-world client requirements, integrating third-party services, and building solutions that improve operational efficiency. By the end of the project, SRVC Hockey had a centralized platform that made camp registration and payment collection significantly easier for both participants and staff.
