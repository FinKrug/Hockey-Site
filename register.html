Section 4 — Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk

I acknowledge that participation in ice hockey and related training activities involves inherent risks including collisions, contact with sticks or pucks, falls, physical exertion injuries, and facility hazards. I voluntarily assume all risks associated with participation in SRVC Hockey Camp.

Section 5 — Release of Liability

I release and hold harmless SRVC Hockey Camp, directors, coaches, staff, volunteers, the host rink, and affiliated organizations from any claims or injuries arising from participation.

Section 6 — Medical Authorization

I certify the participant is physically able to participate. I authorize staff to obtain emergency medical treatment if needed.

Section 7 — Concussion Awareness

I understand hockey carries concussion risks. If a concussion is suspected, the participant will be removed until medically cleared.

Section 8 — Photo & Media Release

I grant permission for SRVC Hockey Camp to photograph or record the participant for promotional and marketing purposes.

Section 9 — Player Code of Conduct

Participants agree to respect coaches, staff, and players; follow safety rules; show sportsmanship; and refrain from bullying or unsafe conduct.

Section 10 — Indemnification Agreement

The participant and/or parent agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the camp and affiliates from any claims or expenses arising from participation.
